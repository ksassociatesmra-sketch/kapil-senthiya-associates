Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before starting a service.

The listed amounts are service fees. Any applicable government, portal, tax or third-party charges will be clarified separately before proceeding.

Is GST registration required for every business?

No. GST registration depends on the applicable law, turnover, type of activity and other conditions. We can discuss your situation before you decide.

Does paying a fee guarantee approval?

No. Final approval and processing are controlled by the relevant government authority. We provide assistance with the stated scope of work.
